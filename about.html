01 / The Artist

MUSIC IS
MY IDENTITY.

Tera Sanki is an Indian Punjabi rapper, singer and lyricist from Gurdaspur, Punjab, known for exploring the evolving sounds of Punjabi hip-hop and contemporary trap music.

With an artistic direction shaped by rap, melody, rhythm and lyrical expression, he aims to create music that connects with listeners while representing his own personality and creative vision.

His musical approach moves between hard-hitting Punjabi rap, melodic vocals and expressive hip-hop sounds. Rather than limiting himself to one style, he continues to experiment and develop a versatile musical identity.

As an independent artist, Tera Sanki is focused on building his presence through original music, consistent creativity and a long-term vision for his career.

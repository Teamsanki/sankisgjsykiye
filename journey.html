Sanki Music Presents / The Artist Story

THE JOURNEY.

Every artist has a story. Mine was never about having everything figured out. It was about holding on to one dream, facing everything that came along the way, and refusing to let that dream disappear.

TERA SANKI — GURDASPUR, PUNJAB, INDIA
Not Just Music

"It was never just about making songs. It was about making a name for myself."

THIS IS MY STORY
01
The Beginning

A DREAM
IN MY HEART.

I come from Gurdaspur, Punjab. Like many young people, I had dreams of building something of my own. But for me, music became more than just an interest. It became a part of who I was.

Rap, melodies, lyrics and beats slowly became my world. I wanted to create music that carried my thoughts, my emotions and my identity.

I didn't have a perfect setup or a ready-made path. I just had the passion to start, the courage to try and a dream that kept getting bigger.

02
The Reality

NOT EVERYONE
UNDERSTOOD.

Following a dream is easy to talk about, but living it is a completely different thing.

There were moments when I had to face questions, criticism and taunts about choosing music as my career. Not everyone believed in what I was trying to build. The support I hoped for was not always there.

Sometimes, the hardest part wasn't making music. It was finding the strength to continue when the people around you couldn't see the future you were imagining.

Financial limitations, career pressure, family expectations and uncertainty made the road even harder. There were days when everything felt overwhelming.

But somewhere inside, I knew I didn't want to give up on something that meant so much to me.

03 / The Independent Grind

IF NO ONE BUILDS IT,
I WILL BUILD IT MYSELF.

I started learning that being an independent artist means much more than writing and singing. There is an entire world behind every release that most people never get to see.

From lyrics and music ideas to recording, production coordination, artwork, video editing, thumbnails, distribution and promotion — I kept learning and handling different parts of my own journey.

01 / Creativity

Writing & Music

Creating lyrics, developing ideas, experimenting with Punjabi rap, trap and melodic sounds.

02 / Development

Learning The Craft

Working on vocals, sound, recording and understanding how to make music better.

03 / Visual Identity

Building The Image

Working on cover artwork, posters, music videos, editing and the visual identity of Tera Sanki.

04 / Independence

Doing It All

Managing releases, distribution, social media and promotion while continuing to develop as an artist.

04
The Music / The Releases

EVERY RELEASE
HAS A STORY.

Every song became another step in understanding myself as an artist. Each release brought new experiences, new challenges and new lessons.

DUNGEON

A dark and fearless track built around the idea of an old prison where jealous rivals and haters are locked away. The message is clear: once they enter this dungeon, there is no way back.

DOOMED

A song about destruction, destiny, and a final journey written in the days of youth. It reflects a darker vision of life, carrying the weight of an ending that feels written long before it arrives.

CARNAGE

A fearless statement aimed at those who stand against me. With bold warnings and an unapologetic attitude, this track represents confidence, fearlessness, and the courage to face every challenge.

NO MERCY

A track driven by brotherhood, loyalty, and fearless energy. Calling my friends brothers and walking with no fear, with Yamdoots by my side, it represents a mindset that refuses to back down.

PYAAR KUDE

A romantic expression born from imagination. Through this track, Tera Sanki sings to a girl who exists in his thoughts, exploring the softer and more melodic side of his musical identity.

TENU DEKHA

Another romantic story shaped by imagination. It captures the moment of seeing someone special, when her smile says more than words ever could. A different story, carrying the same feeling of admiration and attraction.

UNPREDICTABLE

A direct expression of an unpredictable mindset, addressing haters and two-faced friendships. The track reflects thoughts and responses that people never expect, showing a side of Tera Sanki that refuses to follow anyone else's script.

UNEXPECTED

A contrasting story of love and inner conflict. While a girl wants love from me, I find myself searching for hostility in her heart. She carries love within hers, while poison begins to rise inside my chest. An unexpected mix of romance, darkness, and emotion.

BATI BORE

A bold Punjabi hip-hop statement aimed at haters and those who have something to say. The track delivers my response with an aggressive attitude and also pays tribute to my Ustad, Sidhu Moose Wala, through a meaningful reference.

SHIKAR HO GAYA

COMING SOON
A romantic track about unexpectedly falling for someone's eyes. Her surma-filled eyes leave me feeling as if I've been struck right through. A man who was never caught in a police net now finds himself trapped in the beauty of her hair. Sometimes, the most unexpected capture is love.

05 / More Than A Name

TERA
SANKI.

I don't want to be known only by a stage name. I want people to recognise the music, the lyrics, the voice and the identity behind it.

I take inspiration from artists who created their own identity and stayed true to their sound. But my goal is to create my own path, my own style and my own legacy.

I believe real recognition should come from the music, not artificial numbers or temporary popularity.

Independent Artist / Rapper / Singer / Lyricist
06
The Vision

THIS IS
ONLY THE START.

I know I still have a long way to go. There are bigger stages to reach, better music to create, more stories to tell and countless things to learn.

I don't expect everything to happen overnight. I want to grow step by step, improve my craft, build a genuine audience and create music that people can connect with.

The journey is not perfect. The future is uncertain. But the dream is still alive.
